
# 📝 Notee — Your Notes, Your Way

### 💡 Inspiration

I've been using **Goodnotes** for a while and really enjoy its note-taking experience. However, without a subscription, I'm limited to creating only three notes. Since I don't want to pay for a note-taking app, I decided to build my own solution — **Notee**.

### 🚀 My Vision

**Notee** is my attempt to create a simple, accessible, and user-friendly note-taking application that lets me organize my thoughts, manage my notes, and study without subscription restrictions.

### 🎯 Why Notee?

- 📝 **Free Note-Taking:** Create and manage notes without paying for a subscription.
- 🎨 **Simple & Clean UI:** Enjoy a distraction-free writing experience.
- 📚 **Easy Organization:** Keep notes organized and accessible.
- 💡 **Built for Students:** Designed around everyday study and productivity needs.

### 🌱 The Goal

My goal is to build an app that provides a convenient note-taking experience without unnecessary paywalls, while continuously improving it with useful features.
